About Henry Cyril

Henry Cyril is a Principal Systems Architect and researcher with nearly two decades of experience in telecommunications, enterprise systems architecture, service provisioning, network automation, and digital transformation.

His work focuses on the architectural challenges involved in operating high-volume, highly available telecommunications platforms, including transaction consistency, failure recovery, observability, service activation, intelligent automation, and distributed network orchestration.

His research bridges large-scale engineering practice with applied work in event-driven architecture, cloud-native systems, AI-driven anomaly detection, self-healing networks, deterministic transaction processing, and autonomous telecommunications operations.
